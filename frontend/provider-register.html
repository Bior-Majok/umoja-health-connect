Provider Terms & Conditions

Verification requirement

Your account is reviewed by an administrator before you can log in or receive consultations, per Umoja Health Connect's provider verification policy.

Professional conduct

As a provider, you agree to provide accurate medical information, maintain patient confidentiality, respond to assigned consultations in a timely manner, and escalate urgent cases appropriately using the platform's emergency workflow.

Scope of service

Consultations on this platform are limited to teleconsultation and health guidance. Formal diagnosis, prescriptions, and laboratory orders are outside the platform's scope and must be handled through appropriate in-person clinical channels.
