Terms & Conditions

Informed consent

By registering, you consent to Umoja Health Connect collecting and storing your personal health information — name, contact details, age, gender, location, and any symptoms, consultations, appointments, and records you submit — for the purpose of connecting you with verified healthcare providers.

What this platform is — and isn't

Umoja Health Connect provides teleconsultation, appointment scheduling, emergency alerting, and health education. It does not provide medical diagnosis, prescriptions, or laboratory orders. In a life-threatening emergency, use the Emergency Alert feature and seek in-person care immediately.

Your data

Your information is only shared with the healthcare providers and community volunteers directly involved in your care, and with administrators for platform verification and reporting. You may request an update to your profile at any time from your dashboard.
