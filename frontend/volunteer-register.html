Volunteer Terms & Conditions

Acting on behalf of patients

As a community health volunteer, you may submit consultations and trigger emergency alerts on behalf of patients who cannot use the platform directly. You agree to only do so with the patient's knowledge and consent.

Accuracy

You agree to report symptoms and conditions as accurately as possible, based on what the patient tells you or what you directly observe.

Scope of service

Volunteers do not provide medical diagnosis or treatment — your role is to help patients reach a verified healthcare provider through the platform.
